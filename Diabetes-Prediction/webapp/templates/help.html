How do I make a prediction?

Go to New Prediction, fill in all 8 clinical fields, and click Run Diabetes Prediction.

What do the risk colors mean?

Green = low probability, Yellow = moderate, Red = high probability of diabetes. Risk is always shown with a text label as well as a color.

Can I download my results as a PDF?

Yes — from a prediction result, History, or the Reports page, click Generate PDF / Download PDF Report.

I forgot my password.

Use Forgot password? on the login screen and answer your security question.

Is this available as a desktop app too?

Yes — run python app/main.py for the CustomTkinter desktop version, which shares the same database and predictions as this web app.

How do I switch to dark mode?

Use the moon/sun button in the top-right corner. Your preference is remembered on this device.
